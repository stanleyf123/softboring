# History months, soft-report bars, desk edges

No new environment variables. Stripe and email are not required. Locales stay en / zh-tw / ja.

## History by month

History still lists the same weeks. They now sit under the month the account clock shows (or the browser clock for a guest). Order inside a month stays as it arrived. A week with no date rests in one last group. Locked weeks keep their month; the card itself is unchanged.

## Soft report bars

The Soft+ monthly report still shows the four counts: thanks, echoes, gratitudes drawn, and pause weeks. Under them, four cream bars compare those counts with each other. The longest count fills the row. Zero stays empty. A longer bar is not a better month, and the line says so. Free visitors see the same shape with no invented numbers.

## Desk edges

Resting this week and leaving the desk were already possible, and easy to miss.

- The footer links to the pause card. Signed-in members also get a link to leave.
- Account shows both near the top of the desk.
- The pause card points at Account when the desk itself has been enough.

Nothing is emailed. Leaving still asks for `Soft Boring` and the email on the account.
