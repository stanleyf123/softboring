# Guest color key, nickname confirm, filter keys

No new environment variables. Stripe and email are not required. Locales stay `en` / `zh-tw` / `ja`.

## Guest sky and paper key

Guests on the homepage see the five mood colors that can tint a signed-in sky. The sky itself stays the same still picture. On a locked Soft Wall visit, the same key also names the six shared paper colors already on the corkboard. Personal Soft+ washes are not listed there. Paper color is not a feeling score.

## Nickname confirm

Account nickname edits stay pending until the member confirms. A new name, and clearing the name, both wait. An unchanged name does not call the server. A too-short or too-long draft shows the existing hint and does not confirm.

## Soft Wall filter keys

When a Soft+ week chip is focused, arrow keys move the week and Home and End jump to the ends. Away from a text field, `[` and `]` step the lowest feeling, and `{` and `}` (or Shift with those brackets) step the highest. The first step up from empty is 1. One step below 1 clears that side. If the two sides cross, they meet. The keys stay quiet while typing, and while a note dialog is open.
