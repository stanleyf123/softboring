# Soft FAQ, note hover, thank-you history

No new environment variables. Stripe and email are not required. SQLite is unchanged.

## Soft FAQ

Pricing carries a short cream accordion. It says, in plain language, that checkout stays off until payment keys exist, that a gift code can still open Soft+, that weekly reminders are saved but not sent until email is configured, that Free keeps the latest four reviews, and that a wall thank-you never becomes an email. Opening an answer does not start checkout.

## Wall note hover

A readable sticky still rests at five lines. Hover or keyboard focus shows the rest of that same short excerpt, including when reduced motion is on. The open is a reading state, not an animation. Locked and guest notes stay blurred; the expand does not load hidden text.

## Thank-you history

Soft+ sees a short list on Account and on the Soft+ thank-you page: neighbor notes they thanked, newest first. A taken-down note keeps no excerpt. Free and lapsed members see an invitation, not the notes. Nothing is emailed.
