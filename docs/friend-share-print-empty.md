# Friend share, print this week, Soft Wall empties

No new environment variables. Stripe, checkout, Resend, SMTP, and LINE delivery are not involved. Nothing is emailed.

## Friend share card

Signed-in desks (free and Soft+) get a cream card on Account. It copies a public link: the locale home, or `/wall?nick=` when a nickname is already saved — the same pattern as the homepage nickname chips. Three short notes (English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese) are ready to paste. If the browser offers the Web Share API, a second button opens that sheet. There is no prize, no extra Soft+ days, and no outbound email. The older Soft+ invite link, with its quiet star, stays separate.

## Print this week

The open week page has **Print this week** for free and Soft+. It uses the browser print dialog and `@media print`. The sheet is cream paper: week title and the answers already on screen (energy, drain, less of, priorities, feeling, and any custom answers). Choose “Save as PDF” to keep a file. The PNG postcard stays on Soft+.

## Soft Wall empty cards

A wall with zero notes shows a cream card and one next step: write a week, or pin the latest saved week when that week is already yours. When search, feeling, hide-demo, or a week chip leaves nothing, a cream card offers one clear step — clear the filters, or try the other week chip — and mentions `/` and `?` lightly while a filter is on.
